About KAT TURFF

Green Spaces
Built with Care.

KAT TURFF was founded with a single vision — to bring world-class artificial turf solutions to every home, sports facility, and commercial space in Coimbatore. Based in the serene locality of Kovaipudur, we understand what it means to keep spaces green and beautiful all year round.

From premium sports-grade turf for football and cricket courts to low-maintenance residential lawns and decorative landscape grass — we supply, install, and maintain artificial turf with unmatched precision and care.

Serving Kovaipudur, Coimbatore and the surrounding districts, KAT TURFF has earned the trust of hundreds of homes, schools, sports clubs, and corporate clients through consistent quality and honest service.
